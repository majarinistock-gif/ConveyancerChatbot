# Privacy Policy - WhatsApp Conveyancing Bot

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the WhatsApp Conveyancing Bot ("the Service"), a WhatsApp-based chatbot designed to assist Zimbabwean property owners with conveyancing services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our Service.

By using the WhatsApp Conveyancing Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **WhatsApp Phone Number:** Used to identify your account and send/receive messages
- **Name:** Extracted from your WhatsApp profile or ID documents
- **ID Document Details:** National ID number, date of birth, address (extracted via OCR processing)
- **Property Information:** Property descriptions, title deed numbers, subdivision details
- **Contact Information:** Phone number for payment processing via Paynow

### 2.2 Documents and Files
- **Identity Documents:** National ID cards, passports (JPG, PNG, PDF format)
- **Property Documents:** Title deeds, agreements of sale subdivision permits, surveyor general diagrams (PDF format)
- **Legal Documents:** Powers of attorney, affidavits, declarations, clearance certificates (PDF format)

### 2.3 Payment Information
- **Phone Number:** Used for Paynow payment processing (EcoCash, InnBucks, OneMoney)
- **Transaction Details:** Payment status, amount, timestamps

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Information
- **Message Timestamps:** When you send/receive messages
- **Session Data:** Conversation state and progress through the application process
- **Application Metadata:** Service type, selected conveyancer, document upload status

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Service Provision
- Process conveyancing applications through the Deeds Office
- Extract data from ID documents using OCR technology
- Validate document completeness and compliance
- Match you with registered conveyancers
- Facilitate payment processing for administrative fees

### 3.2 Communication
- Send you updates about your application status
- Provide information about required documents
- Respond to your inquiries via WhatsApp
- Send payment confirmations and receipts

### 3.3 Legal Compliance
- Comply with Zimbabwean conveyancing laws and regulations
- Meet Deeds Office documentation requirements
- Maintain records for legal and audit purposes

### 3.4 Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve the Service
- Optimize OCR processing accuracy
- Enhance user experience and conversation flow

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Storage Locations
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas (hosted on AWS, data stored in US-East-1 region)
- **File Storage:** MongoDB GridFS for document storage
- **Payment Processing:** Paynow Zimbabwe (local payment gateway)

### 4.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
- **Access Control:** Restricted access to database and application servers
- **Authentication:** Secure API access tokens for Meta and Paynow integration
- **Regular Backups:** Automated daily backups of all data

### 4.3 Data Retention
- **Active Applications:** Data retained until application is completed or cancelled
- **Completed Applications:** Records retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
- **Failed Applications:** Data deleted after 30 days
- **Session Data:** Automatically expires after 48 hours of inactivity

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 5.1 Service Providers
- **OCR.space:** For document text extraction (documents sent for processing only)
- **Paynow:** For payment processing (phone number and transaction details)
- **Meta Platforms:** For WhatsApp messaging (message content and phone number)
- **MongoDB Atlas:** For database hosting (encrypted storage)

### 5.2 Legal Requirements
- Comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
- Protect our rights, property, and safety
- Prevent fraud or enforce our terms of service

### 5.3 Conveyancers
- Your selected conveyancer will receive your application documents and contact information
- Only conveyancers you explicitly select will have access to your data

## 6. Your Rights

Under Zimbabwean data protection laws and international standards, you have the right to:

### 6.1 Access and Correction
- Request access to your personal information
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- Update your contact details

### 6.2 Data Deletion
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Subject to legal retention requirements for completed applications

### 6.3 Data Portability
- Request a copy of your personal information in a structured format
- Transfer your data to another service provider

### 6.4 Opt-Out
- Opt out of receiving promotional messages (not service-related communications)
- Delete your WhatsApp conversation history at any time

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
